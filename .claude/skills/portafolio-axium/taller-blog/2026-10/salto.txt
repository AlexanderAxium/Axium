Editorial magazine cover photograph.
COMPOSITION: a young person in a loose olive jacket and wide trousers leaps over the camera, shot from directly below against a bright blue sky with soft white clouds; one arm holds a stack of colorful paper sticky notes and a few notes flutter loose in the air. The figure fills the upper part of the frame and is cropped by the edges; the face is turned away or hidden by the arm. The middle band of sky stays clean and calm, room for a headline.
LIGHT: bright midday sun, crisp, a slight overexposure on the clouds.
COLOR: saturated sky blue, sticky notes in cobalt blue, mint teal and soft lavender, olive clothing, warm skin; overall cool and fresh.
CAMERA: 35mm film, 24mm wide angle, extreme low angle, slight motion blur on the fluttering notes, fine grain, landscape 4:3.
AVOID: readable text or writing on the notes, logos, a sharp face looking at the camera, buildings, city, birds, airplanes, the sun disk, lens flare.
