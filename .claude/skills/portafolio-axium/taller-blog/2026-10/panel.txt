Editorial still-life photograph.
COMPOSITION: a close, straight-on view of a clean modern control panel in matte navy-painted metal. A single horizontal row of five chunky metal toggle switches, all flipped up; above each switch a small round indicator light glows soft mint green; under each switch a blank white label plate (no text) waits for a name. To the right of the row, a larger square push button under a flip-up clear safety cover, lit cobalt blue. The row sits in the lower half of the frame; the upper third is plain navy metal with a fine brushed texture, calm space for a headline.
LIGHT: soft studio key light from the top left, subtle specular highlights on the metal, gentle shadows.
COLOR: deep navy (#0B1530) panel, brushed steel switches, mint-green (#7ECFC3) lights, one cobalt-blue (#0072CF) button, white blank labels.
CAMERA: 85mm, straight-on, slight depth of field, crisp detail, landscape 4:3.
AVOID: any letters, numbers, symbols or text on the labels or the panel, logos, screens, warning stickers, red or orange colors, clutter.
